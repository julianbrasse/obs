Why OBS

One school, from Babycare to Upper School.

Bilingual from the start

German and English in equal measure, from Babycare at 3 months to Upper School, so children grow up at home in both languages.

Known as individuals

Small groups, teachers who mentor and a personal coach for each student from Grade 7 onwards, with support for children who need more help and stretch for those ready to go further.

Two pathways, one school

The Swiss Lehrplan 21 up to Grade 9, with two academic tracks, Progymnasium and Gymnasium, under one roof. Then the Swiss Matura or the International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma, without changing school.

Skills for what comes next

The OBS Innovation Academy includes our Space Agency, Ocean & Life Explorers, and Media Academy. Students work on real-world problems, draw on knowledge from several subjects, and develop their own ideas.
